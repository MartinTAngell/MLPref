Informed Consent to Participate in a Research Study

Study title: Perceived Realism of Computer-Generated Water Animations
Principal investigator: Gizem Kayar, New York University
IRB study number: IRB-FY2027-12013

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted at New York University. Please read the information below carefully before deciding whether to participate.

Purpose of the study

The purpose of this study is to understand how people judge the visual realism of computer-generated animations of water. Your responses will help researchers compare different methods for producing such animations.

Procedures

If you agree to participate, you will be shown a series of short, silent video clips of water, each less than 10 seconds long. In each trial you will compare two clips and select the one that better answers the question on the screen; in some trials, a third clip is shown as a reference. At the end, you may optionally indicate your level of experience with 3D computer graphics. The study takes approximately 10 minutes to complete.

Risks and discomforts

There are no known risks associated with this study beyond those encountered in ordinary daily computer use. The videos do not contain flashing or strobing images.

Benefits

There is no direct benefit to you from participating. Your participation will contribute to research on realistic real-time computer graphics.

Compensation

You will not receive any payment or other compensation for participating in this study.

Confidentiality

This study does not collect any information that could identify you. You will not be asked for your name, email address or any other personal details, and your IP address is not recorded. Your responses are stored under a randomly generated code, together with response times and your browser window size. Data are transmitted only if you complete the study, and are stored securely on Google Drive. Results will be reported only in aggregate form.

Voluntary participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to participate, or withdraw at any time without penalty, simply by closing this browser tab. If you withdraw before the end of the study, none of your responses will be recorded. Because no identifying information is collected, responses cannot be withdrawn after they have been submitted.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years of age to participate.

Contact information

If you have questions about this study, please contact mo2273@nyu.edu. If you have questions about your rights as a research participant, you may contact the New York University Institutional Review Board at ask.humansubjects@nyu.edu, (212) 998-4808.

You may print or save a copy of this page for your records.

Statement of consent

By clicking "Yes, I agree" below, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age, that you have read and understood the information above, and that you voluntarily agree to participate in this study.
